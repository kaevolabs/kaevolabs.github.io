Cargo Harbor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you play the Cargo Harbor (Kargo Limanı) mobile game (the "Game"), why it is processed, and what your rights are. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), the data controller is:

  • Mehmet Kaya (Kaevo Labs)
  • Email: kaevolabs@gmail.com

1. In short

  • The Game has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings, captain name) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.
  • We show ads to keep the Game free. Our ad partner Google processes device and advertising data.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.

2. What is processed and why

2.1 Data that stays on your device

Game progress, settings (sound, music, language, effects) and the optional captain name you type are kept in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to third parties and are deleted when you uninstall the Game.

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows Google AdMob ads: interstitial ads between levels and rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example "x2 coins"). For this purpose Google AdMob may process:

  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID),
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/city level),
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, videos watched),
  • device and app diagnostics (model, operating system, crash and performance data).

Purposes: serving and measuring ads, preventing fraud and abuse and, if you allow it, personalised ads.

Legal basis: users in the EU/EEA and the UK are asked for consent to personalised ads through Google's consent form. If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. Elsewhere the basis is our legitimate interest in offering a free service. For users in Turkey, KVKK's processing conditions and its rules on cross-border transfers apply.

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2.3 Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

In-game purchases (for example "No Ads" or coin packs) are made through Google Play. Your payment details stay with Google; we only receive the purchase record (product, time, order number).

We use RevenueCat to verify that purchases are genuine and to restore what you bought (for example No Ads), even on a new device. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated anonymous user ID, your purchase history and basic device information. This ID is not linked to your name or account.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (your purchase) and legal obligations (such as tax records).

2.4 What we do not collect

We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location, SMS or data from other apps. The Game does not request these permissions.

3. Sharing and transfers

Information is shared only with the service providers listed below and only for the purposes described:

Service Purpose Location
Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) Advertising EU, USA and other Google data centres
Google Play (Google LLC) Payments and distribution USA and other
RevenueCat, Inc. Purchase verification USA

Because these providers are located abroad, your data is transferred internationally, relying on the safeguards of GDPR and Article 9 of KVKK (such as standard contractual clauses). We do not sell your data.

4. Retention

  • Game data on your device: until you uninstall the Game.
  • Advertising data: according to Google's retention periods.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to restore your purchases and to meet legal obligations.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR and Article 11 of KVKK you may:

  • ask whether your data is processed and obtain information about it;
  • request correction or deletion;
  • object to processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • complain to a supervisory authority: your local data protection authority in the EU/EEA, or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

What you can do right away:

  • Ad consent: change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Game data: uninstalling the Game deletes all game data on your device.
  • Purchase records: for deletion or information requests email kaevolabs@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

6. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Game progress stays on your device. Communication with service providers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Purchases are verified server-side.

8. Changes

This policy will be updated when new features are added (for example online leaderboards). Significant changes are announced in the Game. The current version is always available at this address.
