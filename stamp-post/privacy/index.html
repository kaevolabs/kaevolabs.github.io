Stamp Post — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026 (first version: 20 October 2026)

This policy explains what information is processed when you play the Stamp Post (Mühür Postası) mobile game (the "Game"), why it is processed, and what your rights are. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), the data controller is:

  • Mehmet Kaya (Kaevo Labs)
  • Email: kaevolabs@gmail.com

1. In short

  • The Game has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
  • Your progress (stamps, stars, album, points, settings, player name and country) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us. We run no server of our own.
  • We show ads to keep the Game free. Our ad partner Google processes device and advertising data.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your scores for the leaderboards are kept by Google.
  • The Game contains no analytics of its own.

2. What is processed and why

2.1 Data that stays on your device

Game progress (finished stamps, stars, album, points, rank, boosters), settings (sound, music, language, desk colour, reminders) and the player name and country you choose are kept in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to third parties and are deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows Google AdMob ads: occasional full-screen ads after a finished stamp and rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example to get a booster or to continue a stamp). No ads are shown in the middle of a stamp. For this purpose Google AdMob may process:

  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID),
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/city level),
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, videos watched),
  • device and app diagnostics (model, operating system, language, crash and performance data).

Purposes: serving and measuring ads, preventing fraud and abuse and, if you allow it, personalised ads.

Legal basis: users in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent to personalised ads through Google's consent form. If you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown. Elsewhere the basis is our legitimate interest in offering a free service. For users in Turkey, KVKK's processing conditions and its rules on cross-border transfers apply. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2.3 Purchases (Google Play)

In-game purchases ("Remove ads" and booster packs such as erasers, lenses, pencils and the post bag) are made through Google Play Billing. Your payment details stay with Google. The Game only learns from Google Play which products you own or have just bought, so it can turn ads off or add the boosters. Google Play keeps the purchase record (product, time, order number).

Legal basis: performance of the contract (your purchase) and legal obligations (such as tax records).

2.4 Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

The Game can show two leaderboards: Stamp of the Day (mistakes and time on the day's first attempt) and weekly points. They are run by Google Play Games; scores are sent to Google, not to us. Using Play Games is optional and the Game is fully playable without it. If you are signed in, Google Play Games processes:

  • your Play Games profile (player name and picture) and player ID,
  • your scores and rank in this game,
  • Google's own service diagnostics and analytics.

Your Play Games name and scores are visible to other players according to your Play Games profile visibility setting. The player name you type in the Game is not sent. The Game can only read this game's leaderboards; it cannot access your data from other games.

Purpose: to provide the leaderboards. Legal basis: performance of a service you asked for.

You can manage or delete your profile and game data in your Play Games profile or Google Account: https://play.google.com/games/profile

2.5 Reminders

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules reminders (for example the evening reminder for the Stamp of the Day, or an event you asked to be reminded about) on your device. No data leaves your device for this. You can turn reminders off in the Game's settings or in Android settings.

2.6 Feedback by email

Settings → Send feedback opens your own email app with our address and the Game's version in the subject line. We only receive what you choose to send, use it only to answer you and improve the Game, and delete it on request.

2.7 What we do not collect

We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location, SMS or data from other apps. The Game does not request these permissions. Google Play may show us aggregated crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) from users who agreed to share them with Google; these do not identify you.

3. Sharing and transfers

Information is shared only with the service providers listed below and only for the purposes described:

Service Purpose Location
Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) Advertising EU, USA and other Google data centres
Google Play (Google LLC) Payments and distribution USA and other
Google Play Games (Google LLC) Leaderboards USA and other

Because these providers are located abroad, your data is transferred internationally, relying on the safeguards of GDPR and Article 9 of KVKK (such as standard contractual clauses). We do not sell your data.

4. Retention

  • Game data on your device: until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Advertising data: according to Google's retention periods.
  • Leaderboard data: until you delete your Play Games profile or game data (kept by Google).
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play as needed to restore your purchases and to meet legal obligations.
  • Feedback emails: until the matter is closed, or earlier on request.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR and Article 11 of KVKK you may:

  • ask whether your data is processed and obtain information about it;
  • request correction or deletion;
  • object to processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • complain to a supervisory authority: your local data protection authority in the EU/EEA, or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.

What you can do right away:

  • Ad consent: change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Game data: uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes all game data on your device.
  • Leaderboards: delete this game's data or hide your profile in your Play Games profile.
  • Anything else: email kaevolabs@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

6. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Game progress stays on your device. Communication with service providers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes

If the Game gains new online features (such as cloud saves or analytics), this policy is updated before they go live and the date above changes. Significant changes are announced in the Game. The current version is always available at this address.
